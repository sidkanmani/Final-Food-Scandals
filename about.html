Food Scandals · About the Project

What is this?

Food Scandals is a website about scandals, drama, controversies, and debacles related to food. Personal questions like "was New York Style cheesecake invented in Florida?" and "Isn't it kinda Islamophobic that we couldn't find tuna DNA in the Subway tuna sandwich?" are what inspired this project.

This website was made as part of an Introduction to Web Development midterm and final at NYU's Interactive Telecommunications Program, within the advanced certificate in digital humanities. The hope of the assignment was to create a static website relevant to your academic research interests. Food Scandals is that, and more.

Have any leads on a juicy, funny, or downright messy and outrageous food scandal? Spill!

Food Scandals · About the Dramatist

Sidikha Ashraf

Sidikha Ashraf is a writer, comedian, dramaturg, and evolving artist currently doing her masters at NYU's Interactive Telecommunications Program. She did not pursue this masters for the purpose of academia — she comes from a performance art, theater, and creative writing background. That brings us to Food Scandals.

If there's anything Sidikha has researched in her free time, it's scandals and conundrums in the news related to food. She's just a girl, a South Asian American Muslim, 28-year-old doing her masters with a lot to say about the food scandals she comes across.

If only Food Scandals was a comedic docuseries starring Sidikha. Sound like a good idea? Know how to make that happen? Well then, Spill!
